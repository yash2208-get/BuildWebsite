FAQ

Questions, answered

Can I export my website?

On eligible plans you can export clean, dependency-free HTML and CSS at any time. Your content is always yours.

How does the AI generator work?

Describe your business in a sentence or two and the generator composes a full multi-section site — copy, layout, palette and imagery direction included. Everything remains fully editable.

What happens when I hit my plan limits?

Nothing breaks. We notify you before you reach a limit and you can upgrade at any time — changes apply instantly and are pro-rated.

Can I use my own domain?

Yes. Connect any domain you own from the dashboard and we handle DNS verification and TLS certificates automatically.

Is there a free trial?

Every paid plan includes a no-risk trial. No card is required to start building, and you can cancel with one click.
